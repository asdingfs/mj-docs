## A small step is a good start

Pick one experience. Explain what you did, what you learned, and what you would try next. Add a relevant image or piece of work, then preview your Chapter.

Use a desktop screen for Chapter editing. You can still preview Chapters on smaller devices.

**Journey tip:** A focused Chapter is easier to build today and easier to reuse tomorrow.
