# What is MyJourney?

MyJourney helps you turn your learning, projects, and experiences into Chapters you can share and bring together into Stories.

## Chapters capture an experience

A Chapter is a page you build with text, images, media, files, and records from your available resources. You might use one for a design project, a moment of discovery, or something you learned while working with a team.

[Create your first Chapter](../features/my-chapters/creating-a-chapter.md).

## Stories connect the pieces

A Story brings selected Chapters together for a particular audience. The same Chapter can appear in more than one Story, so you can choose a different collection for an internship introduction or an exchange application.

[Explore My Stories](../features/my-stories/README.md).

## Sharing is a separate step

Creating a Chapter or Story does not make it public. Publish the Chapters you want readers to see, and publish the Story if you want to share them as a collection. Copying a share link alone does not publish your work.

[See how publishing works](../features/my-stories/publishing-and-sharing-a-story.md).
