# My Stories

## Introduction

A **Story** is a personally curated and meaningful collection of one or more **Chapters**, shaped for the people you want to reach by your personal values, belief, and what matters to you

It is where the different _dots_ you have collected (i.e. projects, pivotal moments, sparks of inspiration, lessons from failure, or chance encounters that became passion project) begin to **connect**. Together, we want your story to reveal a **coherent narrative** about what you believe in, where your strengths lie, what you value, and what you want to bring to the world!

Creating a story is also an opportunity for **reflection and clarity**. By revisiting the experiences captured in your Chapters, you can uncover patterns, distill their meaning, and understand how they have shaped you. With this clearer perspective, you can then choose your **next act with greater intention**. It's like a positive feedback loop that will perpetually bring you closer to a life that's personally meaningful to you!

Every story should be **curated** for its audience. Choosing the right experiences and finding the right voice for the people you want to reach can increase the relevance, impact, and reach of your Story. Your experiences are always there inside of you, but how you tell the story of these experiences can, and should, change with the audience and the opportunity, so it resonates with the people you most want to reach.

## Stories and Chapters

A **Chapter** can belong to one or more Stories. When you update a Chapter, the changes are reflected across every Story that contains it. If you need to adapt its content for a particular audience, you can create a separate copy by **duplicating** the Chapter.

{% hint style="info" %}
Curating your story is a lot easier when you have already captured a number of meaningful chapters in [my-chapters](../my-chapters/ "mention")! That's why MyJourney team highly encourages you to take small, but consistent & frequent steps to reflect on, and clarify the story of your life, one chapter at a time!
{% endhint %}

When an opportunity arises, simply select, reuse, and arrange the relevant Chapters for your chosen audience!

### A simple way to begin

1. Think about who will read your Story.
2. Decide what you want them to understand about you.
3. Select the chapters that make that message shine.

Your journey has many sides. My Stories encourages you share the right one, at the right time.

### Make each story feel right

Choose the chapters that show what matters most. Add a clear title. Keep the focus on the message you want someone to remember.

For example, you could create:

* **Portfolio for employers** - projects, skills, and results that show how you work.
* **Finding my passion** - key moments that led you towards a field or cause.
* **Exchange application** - experiences that show curiosity, adaptability, and cultural openness.
* **Internship introduction** - relevant learning, teamwork, and goals for your next step.
* **My leadership journey** - times you guided a team, started something, or made change happen.
* **From challenge to growth** - setbacks that taught you resilience and new skills.

One chapter can belong in many Stories. A group project may show teamwork for an internship. The same project may show creativity in a portfolio. So, pick the angle that fits your audience!
