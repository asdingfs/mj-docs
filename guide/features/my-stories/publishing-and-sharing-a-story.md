# Publishing and sharing a Story

Ready to show someone your journey? Check both the Story and the Chapters inside it.

## Publish and copy the link

1. Open each Chapter you want readers to see and [publish its content](../my-chapters/publishing-and-sharing-a-chapter-draft.md).
2. Return to **My Stories** and [select the Story](selecting-a-story-to-work-on.md).
3. Check its title, cover, and [Chapter selection and order](managing-chapters-inside-a-story.md).
4. Select the visibility control labelled **Publish story** on the header. After publishing, its label changes to **Unpublish story**.
5. Select the chain-link icon labelled **Share link**.
6. Open the copied link in a signed-out browser window to check the reader's view before sending it.

## Why is a Chapter missing?

A published Story shows only published Chapters. Check that the Chapter is in the Story's saved list and that the Chapter itself is published. Copying either share link does not change its publication state.

## Take a Story offline

Select **Unpublish story** on its header. The Story's public link will stop showing the collection.

Unpublishing a Story does not unpublish its Chapters. Their individual links can still work. To take a Chapter offline too, [unpublish that Chapter](../my-chapters/publishing-and-sharing-a-chapter-draft.md).

**Before sharing:** Published links are public. Include only work and information you intend your readers to see.
