# Customising a Story's header

Help readers understand your Story before they open its first Chapter. The Story cover is the landscape image in this header.

## Edit the header and cover

1. [Select the Story](selecting-a-story-to-work-on.md) you want to edit.
2. Select the pencil icon labelled **Edit story** on its header.
3. Update the **Title**, **Description**, or **Headline**. Keep the description within the on-screen character counter.
4. Under **Cover image (optional)**, select **Choose image** to add a cover, or **Change image** to replace one.
5. Choose an image. In **Adjust image crop**, move or resize the **16:9** landscape selection. Use **Smaller selection**, **Larger selection**, or **Reset**, then inspect **Selected crop** and the wide and narrow header display examples.
6. Select **Use cropped image** to return to Edit Story with the new thumbnail.
7. Select **Save** and wait for the upload and save to finish.

The title names your collection, the description explains its focus, and the headline adds a short introduction beside your profile information.

## Review before saving

Accepting the crop prepares your cover; it does not apply it to the Story until you save. **Discard change** restores the previous cover preview. **Cancel** in the crop dialog leaves the previous selection unchanged, and closing Edit Story before saving leaves the saved Story unchanged.

Only the selected area is saved as a still image. The header can trim it differently across screen sizes, so keep important subjects near the centre.

**Before changing a published Story:** Saving the header text or cropped cover can update the public Story immediately. [Unpublish the Story](publishing-and-sharing-a-story.md) first if you need to work privately. Its cover does not change the covers or banners of its Chapters.
