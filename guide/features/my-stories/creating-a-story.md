# Creating a Story

Give your next collection a purpose: what would you like its reader to understand about you?

1. Open **My Stories** and select **Create Story** beside the Story selector. On a narrow screen, this control may show only a plus icon.
2. Enter a **Title**. Optionally add a **Description** and **Headline** to introduce your collection.
3. To add an optional Story cover, select **Choose image** and choose a landscape image from your device.
4. In **Adjust image crop**, move or resize the **16:9** selection. Check **Selected crop** and the wide and narrow header display examples. **Smaller selection**, **Larger selection**, and **Reset** help you adjust the framing.
5. Select **Use cropped image** to return to Create Story with the cover thumbnail.
6. Select **Create** and wait for the cover upload and Story creation to finish.

Only the selected crop is saved as a still image. Header shapes vary with screen size, so keep your main subject near the centre.

The cover is optional. **Cancel** in the crop dialog leaves the previous selection unchanged; **Discard** in Create Story clears the pending cover. You can also use **Change image** to choose and crop a replacement before creating the Story.

Your new Story opens so you can start curating it. It is not public until you publish it. The Story cover is its header image; it is separate from the covers and banners of its Chapters.
