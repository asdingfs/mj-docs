# Managing Chapters inside a Story

Choose the experiences that belong together, then arrange them in the order you want readers to explore.

1. [Select your Story](selecting-a-story-to-work-on.md).
2. Select **Manage Chapters**, above the Chapter cards.
3. Move Chapters from **Available Chapters** into **Current Story Chapters**. Use the add control on a Chapter or drag it into the current list.
4. Drag Chapters within the current list to change their order.
5. Use **Remove** to take a Chapter out of this Story.
6. Select **Save Changes** to apply your selection and order. **Cancel** leaves the saved collection unchanged.

On smaller screens, use the **Current** and **Available** tabs to switch lists.

## What changes, and what stays?

- Removing a Chapter from a Story keeps it in **My Chapters**.
- Adding an existing Chapter reuses it; it does not make an independent copy.
- Creating a new Chapter while you are inside a Story adds it to that Story automatically.
- A public Story shows only its published Chapters. Adding a draft does not publish it.
- Saving the collection changes a published Story's contents and order immediately.
