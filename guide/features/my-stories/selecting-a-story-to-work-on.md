# Selecting a Story to work on

Select the Story you want to change before adding Chapters or editing its header.

1. Open **My Stories**.
2. Open the Story selector above the large header image. It shows the current Story's title.
3. Use **Search stories** if you need to narrow the list.
4. Select the Story you want. Check the title in the selector and header before making changes.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/01.jpg" alt="My Stories page with the Story selector above the header and Create Story to its right."><figcaption>The wide selector above the header identifies the Story you are working on. Example content will differ from your own.</figcaption></figure>

If the selector says **No stories yet**, [create a Story](creating-a-story.md).

A Chapter created from the selected Story is added to that Story automatically. A Chapter created from **My Chapters** can be added later through [Manage Chapters](managing-chapters-inside-a-story.md).
