# My Chapters

A Chapter gives one experience room to breathe. Explain what happened, show your work, and reflect on what you learned.

Open **My Chapters** from the top navigation, or from the menu on a smaller screen. Use **Search chapters** to find a Chapter by its title.

## Start or continue a Chapter

- [Create a Chapter](creating-a-chapter.md) for a new experience.
- On a desktop, hover over an existing Chapter card and select **Edit Draft** to continue working on its content.
- Select **Edit Chapter** to change its title or choose, crop, and save its card cover. This action is also available in the three-dot **Chapter actions** menu.
- Select **Preview** to view the Chapter. On a smaller device, open the three-dot **Chapter actions** menu to find preview and sharing controls.

Chapter editing needs a desktop screen for the canvas and settings panels. A smaller device can still open a preview.

## Understand the card labels

**Published** means a public version is available. **Draft** means a draft version exists. You may see both labels when a published Chapter also has a draft.

Saving a draft and publishing it are different actions. Read [publishing and sharing](publishing-and-sharing-a-chapter-draft.md) before sending a link.
