# Chapter cover and banner image

Your Chapter has two image roles. Choose each for the place it will appear.

| Image | Where readers see it | Where you change it | Crop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chapter cover | Chapter cards in My Chapters and Stories | Create Chapter or Edit Chapter | 3:4 portrait |
| Chapter banner | Top of the opened Chapter | Canvas Settings → Chapter Banner | 16:9 landscape |

Changing a dedicated card cover does not replace the banner, and changing the banner does not replace a dedicated card cover. If a Chapter has no dedicated cover, its card can use the banner as a fallback.

## Add or change the card cover

Choose and crop a cover while [creating your Chapter](creating-a-chapter.md), or open **Edit Chapter** from its card to replace it later. See [Customising a Chapter's cover image](customising-chapters-cover-image.md) for the complete steps.

## Add or change the Chapter banner

1. Open **Edit Draft** for the Chapter on a desktop.
2. Select the Chapter title on the canvas to open **Canvas Settings**.
3. Expand **Chapter Banner**.
4. Select **Choose banner image**, or **Change banner image** if one is already present.
5. Choose an image from your device. The **Adjust image crop** dialog opens with a **16:9** landscape selection.
6. Move or resize the highlighted area. Use **Smaller selection**, **Larger selection**, and **Reset** as needed. Check **Selected crop** and the wide and narrow header display examples.
7. Select **Use cropped image**, then wait for the banner upload to finish. **Cancel** leaves the current banner unchanged.
8. Adjust the banner settings, select **Save**, and open **Preview** to check the result.

Only the selected area is saved as a still image. The displayed header may trim that crop differently at different screen sizes, so keep important subjects near the centre.

## Adjust the banner's appearance

- **Height** changes its height.
- **Shadow** changes the available shadow treatment.
- **Show banner image** controls whether it appears inside the Chapter.
- **Title overlays banner** places the title over the banner rather than displaying it separately.

Hiding the banner does not remove the card cover.

**If the Chapter is already public:** Accepting a banner crop starts its upload and applies the banner when that succeeds. The banner and saved canvas settings can affect the public Chapter before you publish content updates. [Unpublish the Chapter](publishing-and-sharing-a-chapter-draft.md) first if those changes need to stay private.
