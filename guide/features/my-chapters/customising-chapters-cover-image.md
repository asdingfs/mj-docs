# Customising a Chapter's cover image

Your card cover helps readers recognise a Chapter in **My Chapters** and **My Stories**. It uses a **3:4 portrait crop** and is separate from the banner inside the Chapter.

## Choose, crop, and save a replacement

1. Find the Chapter card in **My Chapters** or in a Story.
2. On a desktop, hover over the card and select **Edit Chapter**. On a smaller device, open the three-dot **Chapter actions** menu and select **Edit Chapter**.
3. In **Cover image (optional)**, select **Change image** if a cover is shown, or **Choose image** if there is none. You can also update the **Title** here.
4. Choose an image. In **Adjust image crop**, move or resize the **3:4** selection to frame your subject.
5. Check **Selected crop**. Use **Smaller selection**, **Larger selection**, or **Reset** if needed.
6. Select **Use cropped image** to return to **Edit Chapter** with the new thumbnail.
7. Select **Save** and wait for the upload and save to finish.

Selecting **Use cropped image** prepares the replacement; **Save** applies it to the Chapter. The saved crop is a still image.

## Change your mind before saving

**Cancel** in the crop dialog leaves your previous selection in place. **Discard change** in Edit Chapter removes the pending replacement and restores the previous cover preview. Closing Edit Chapter before saving leaves the saved details unchanged.

Replacing a cover changes the Chapter card wherever it is used, including in a published Story. It does not replace a dedicated Chapter banner.

For the wide image at the top of the opened Chapter, use [Chapter Banner settings](chapter-cover-and-banner-image.md).
