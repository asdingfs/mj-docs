# Text tools

Give your work a little context: what did you try, what changed, and what did you learn?

1. Open **Design Tools** in the Chapter editor.
2. Select **Text**, or drag it onto the canvas.
3. Select the Text block and type your content.
4. Select the text you want to format, then use the Text Block controls in **Settings**.

## Shape your text

Use **Typography** to choose a font and size, apply bold, italic, underline, or strikethrough, and adjust alignment. Subscript and superscript are available for text that needs them.

Use **Colour** to change text colour or add a highlight. Keep enough contrast for your words to remain easy to read.

The Text Block controls also include **Undo** and **Redo**. These controls apply to text editing; do not rely on them to recover a removed block.

## A reflection you can build on

Start with three short paragraphs:

- **The challenge:** What were you trying to understand or make?
- **Your contribution:** What did you do, and why?
- **What you learned:** What would you repeat or change next time?

Select **Save**, wait for **Saved**, and open **Preview** to check the result. [Publish updates](../publishing-and-sharing-a-chapter-draft.md) when your revised content is ready for readers.
