# Publishing and sharing a Chapter

Give your Chapter a final read before inviting someone into your journey.

## Save, preview, and publish

1. Open **Edit Draft** on a desktop.
2. Select **Save** and wait for **Saved**.
3. Open **Preview**. Check your wording, images, and links.
4. Return to the editor and select **Publish**. If a public version already exists, select **Publish updates**.
5. Read the confirmation and select **Publish** or **Publish updates** again.
6. Return to the Chapter card and select **Share Link**. On a smaller device, use **Chapter actions** and **Copy Share Link**.
7. Open the copied link in a signed-out window to check the public result.

## What do readers see?

The public page shows the latest published content blocks. Saving a draft does not publish those revised blocks. Publishing updates replaces the content version readers see at the shared link.

The title, card cover, banner image, and canvas settings are shared Chapter details. Changes to these can affect an already-public Chapter before you publish its revised content. Card covers are cropped and applied through **Edit Chapter → Save**; banners are cropped and uploaded from **Chapter Banner** in the editor. Unpublish first if you need all of those changes to stay private.

Copying a link does not publish the Chapter. An unpublished Chapter's link will not show its content to readers.

## Unpublish a Chapter

Use **Unpublish chapter** on its card, or **Unpublish** in the Chapter actions menu, then confirm. Its public link stops showing the Chapter, and it no longer appears in public Stories. Your draft remains available for editing.

## If saving needs attention

- **Save failed:** Keep the tab open, check the message, and use **Retry** when available.
- **Offline:** Reconnect and make sure saving succeeds before leaving.
- **Session ended:** Another tab has taken over editing. Check the other tab before reloading or taking over; unsaved changes may be lost.

To share several Chapters as a collection, [publish a Story](../my-stories/publishing-and-sharing-a-story.md).
