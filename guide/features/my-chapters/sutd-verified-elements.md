# Resources and SUTD verified elements

Bring relevant records and files into your Chapter from the editor's **Resources** panel.

1. Open your Chapter on a desktop.
2. Use **Expand Sources** on the left if the panel is collapsed.
3. Choose **Creations**, **Academics**, or **Achievements**.
4. Browse the available collections and records. Use **Search files** where it is shown.
5. Add the relevant available item to your Chapter, then check how it appears in **Preview**.

## Read the verification label carefully

Some source records display **SUTD Verified**. Look for the badge on the specific record; adding an ordinary Text or Image block does not give it that status.

Source records preserve information from their source. Some controls are intentionally limited: for example, a verified Dive record can be reordered or removed, rather than rewritten in the Chapter editor.

A verification badge on a record does not mean that your whole Chapter or Story has been institutionally approved.

If a record is missing or incorrect, check the original source information before using it. This guide does not define an institutional verification-request or correction process.

**Journey tip:** Put your reflection beside a record to explain what it means to you, while keeping the record itself intact.
